# 09: A week on the device with the spare number

**What to build:** The T-Deck Max used as the spare number's WhatsApp for a
week of ordinary use, before the main number is linked. This is by hand,
and its results go in Comments.

**Blocked by:** 05, 06, 07

**Status:** ready-for-human

- [ ] Linked both by QR and by pairing code
- [ ] Messages arrive from light sleep within one interval, and alert as the spec says
- [ ] Internal RAM free during a check and with the app open, with and without the tailnet on
- [ ] Battery drain over a day at the 15-minute interval, against a day with WhatsApp off
- [ ] Still linked and not banned after a week; then, and only then, the main number may be linked
